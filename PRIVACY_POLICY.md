### Privacy Policy for CST/IST Time Converter

This Chrome Extension ("CST/IST Time Converter") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or user data of any kind. All time conversion calculations are performed locally within your browser. We do not use analytics, and we do not share any information with third parties.
